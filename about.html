Our Story

Weddings by Kublack is a branch of Ku Black Photography, dedicated entirely to one thing: telling your love story exactly as it happens — through your engagement, your wedding day, and every couple shoot in between.

It started with a simple belief: that a wedding day moves too fast to be staged. Over more than 5 years and 20+ weddings a year, we've learned to work quietly within the day rather than direct it — staying close enough to catch the moment, and out of the way enough to let it happen naturally.

That approach has earned the trust of more than 40 couples, and it's still the same one we bring to every wedding we shoot today. Not just pictures — the smiles, the tears, the in-between moments you'd otherwise forget you had.
